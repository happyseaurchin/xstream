# Coordinate Sign Exploration

The sign of coordinate VALUES (±321) — distinct from pscale position within a semantic-number.

---

## The Problem

We've said "negative pscale = rules/governance" but this conflates two things:

1. **Pscale position** — place value (10^n) within a semantic-number
2. **Coordinate sign** — whether the value itself is positive or negative

A semantic-number like `321` has pscale 0 (rightmost digit). But what does `-321` mean? The negative sign applies to the whole number, not to a pscale position.

---

## What ± Might Mean

| Interpretation | Positive | Negative | Zero |
|----------------|----------|----------|------|
| **Ontological** | Real/settled/actual | Fictional/projected/imagined | Transform boundary |
| **Temporal** | Past (settled) | Future (projected) | Present moment |
| **Spatial** | Here (real) | There (fictional) | The membrane |
| **Identity** | Self (real person) | Character (fictional) | The relationship |

---

## The Relativistic Insight

Every entity has its own origin (0,0,0). Coordinates are relative to observer.

| Observer | Their 0,0,0 | URB coordinates | Wales coordinates |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| User (David) | David, Wales, now | s: -321 (fictional) | s: +321 (real) |
| Character (Marcus) | Marcus, URB, now | s: +321 (real to him) | s: -321 (mythical) |

**The elegant part**: This mirrors consciousness. Your character experiences their world as real. "Fictional" only appears from external frame.

**Transform**: David → Marcus inverts sign. The character's positive IS the user's negative.

---

## The Membrane Model

Zero isn't a point but a boundary:

```
    REFRACTED (negative)          REFLECTED (positive)
          ←───────────────|0|───────────────→
    fictional places      │      real places
    future/projected      │      past/settled
    characters           │      real people
                         │
                DEFRACTED (the membrane)
                transforms between frames
```

You don't navigate "in" zero. You define transforms across it.

---

## Where Do Rules Live?

**Option A: Rules have scope, not position**

A rule doesn't live "at" s:-2. It has `scope: s:300-399` meaning "applies to content in this range." Frame-independent infrastructure.

**Option B: Rules distinguished by shelf**

Content and rules can both be at s:+321. Rules have `shelf: 'rule'` vs `shelf: 'solid'`.

**Option C: Imaginary axis**

```
s: 321 + 0i    → content at spatial 321
s: 321 + 1i    → rule applying to spatial 321
```

Mathematical elegance but may be over-engineering.

---

## Current Working Hypothesis

Combine relativistic frames + membrane + shelf distinction:

1. **Every entity has relative 0,0,0** — coordinates are "from here"
2. **Sign = ontological status** — positive is "real to this observer"
3. **Zero = transform boundary** — not navigable, defines frame relationships
4. **Rules distinguished by property** — scope field or shelf value, not coordinate sign
